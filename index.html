Alberta Class 7 Test Prep

Practise for the Alberta Class 7 learner's licence knowledge test on your iPhone, with 20 mock exams of 30 questions each, road-sign study, diagram practice and short lessons.

Disclaimer: Alberta Class 7 Test Prep is an independent study aid by ChowChow Labs. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Government of Alberta, Alberta Transportation and Economic Corridors, or any registry agent.

Features

Free: Mock Exams 1–7, topic practice using those exams' questions, sign study and lessons. Free users see Google AdMob ads, and the app asks permission through Apple's tracking prompt before personalised ads.

Unlock with ads: any locked mock exam (8–20) can be opened for 24 hours by choosing to watch 3 rewarded ads for it, and progress is saved between ads. Free users can also watch a rewarded ad to reveal more answer explanations, or 3 ads for 24 hours of full Progress.

Premium: unlocks Mock Exams 8–20, Smart Practice across all 600 questions, full history and review collections, and removes ads.

Each mock has 30 questions with a target of 25 correct, matching the published Alberta test format. Questions are original and based on publicly available Alberta driving rules; they are not official test questions. All content is bundled in the app, with no server and no accounts. Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Alberta.

Premium One-Time: $9.99 (CAD in Canada, USD in the US), a one-time purchase that does not renew.

Premium Monthly: $2.99 per month (CAD in Canada, USD in the US), an auto-renewing subscription charged to your Apple ID, with no free trial. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions on your iPhone, or use Manage Subscription in the app's Settings. Restore Purchases is available on the Premium screen and in Settings.
