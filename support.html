Frequently asked questions

What is free and what is Premium?

Mock Exams 1–7 are free, along with topic practice, sign study and lessons. Premium unlocks Mock Exams 8–20, Smart Practice across all 600 questions, full history and review collections, and removes ads.

How does unlocking an exam with ads work?

Open a locked exam (8–20), choose to watch 3 rewarded ads for it, and it unlocks for 24 hours. An ad only counts if you watch it to the end, and your progress is saved between ads. An unlocked exam can be retaken as often as you like during the 24 hours.

How much is Premium and how do I cancel?

Premium One-Time is $9.99 and does not renew. Premium Monthly is $2.99 per month (CAD in Canada, USD in the US) with no free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID. The monthly subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. To cancel or manage it, open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions on your iPhone, or tap Manage Subscription in the app's Settings. Buying one-time Premium does not cancel an existing monthly subscription.

How do I restore Premium?

Open the app and use "Restore Purchases" on the Premium screen or in Settings. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Why do I see ads?

The free version shows Google AdMob ads. Premium removes them. Before personalised ads are shown, the app asks your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Are the questions the same as on the real test?

No. The questions are original and based on publicly available Alberta driving rules, written to help you study. The official test may differ, and practice results don't guarantee a pass. Always refer to the official Alberta Driver's Guide for the authoritative rules of the road.

How do I delete my data?

Your progress is stored on your device only. Use Settings > Reset study history in the app, or delete the app.
