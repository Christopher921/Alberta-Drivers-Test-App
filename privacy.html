Privacy Policy

App: Alberta Class 7 Test Prep  |  Last updated: October 31, 2026

Disclaimer: Alberta Class 7 Test Prep is an independent study aid by ChowChow Labs. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Government of Alberta, Alberta Transportation and Economic Corridors, or any registry agent.

Overview

Alberta Class 7 Test Prep ("the app") helps users practise for the Alberta Class 7 learner's licence knowledge test. This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect.

Information we collect

We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or other personal details inside the app. Your answers, results, flags, ad-unlock progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob: banner ads, a full-screen ad when you finish a mock exam or practice set, and rewarded ads that you choose to watch (to unlock a mock exam for 24 hours, reveal an answer explanation, or open full Progress for 24 hours). AdMob may collect and use device identifiers and usage data (such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and ad interaction data) to serve and measure ads. You can limit ad personalization in your device's Settings (Privacy & Security > Tracking, and Apple Advertising). For more information, see Google's advertising policies and Google's Privacy Policy.

On iOS, the app asks your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before AdMob may use the advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalised ads. If you decline, ads are not personalised using the IDFA. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your ad choices, and you can change them later in the app's Settings. Premium users see no ads.

Premium purchases

The app offers Premium as an optional one-time purchase or an auto-renewing monthly subscription. All payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details; we receive only Apple's confirmation of your entitlement to Premium.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the third-party services described above (Google AdMob for ads and Apple for purchases), which operate under their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for people preparing for a driver's knowledge test (Alberta learners can apply from age 14) and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data is stored on your device, you can remove it at any time with Settings > Reset study history in the app, or by deleting the app. We operate no server that stores your data, so there is no server-side data to delete.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do.
